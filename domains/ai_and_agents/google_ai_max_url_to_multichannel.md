## Pattern

Google AI Max takes a URL and a target ROAS, then automatically deploys campaigns across Search, Discover, Gmail, YouTube Shorts, and Maps within 24 hours. Google's published data shows +18% unique query categories converting and +19% overall conversions versus manually configured campaigns.

This is Google's answer to Meta's full-automation push — reducing campaign setup to a single input (URL + objective) and letting the system handle creative generation, targeting, placement, and optimization across channels.

## Apply When

- Client has a product catalog or well-structured landing pages
- Evaluating whether to maintain siloed channel setups vs. consolidated AI-driven campaigns
- TNT Growth client onboarding — AI Max as a fast-start option for new accounts
- Comparing against Meta Advantage+ for cross-platform strategy

## Boundaries

- Performance data is Google-published — independent verification needed as adoption scales
- Works best with well-structured, crawlable landing pages (URL is the primary input)
- May not suit clients needing granular channel-level budget control

## What Changed

Siloed channel campaign setups become harder to justify when a single URL input can deploy across 5 channels with competitive performance. Changes the default recommendation for new Google campaigns.
