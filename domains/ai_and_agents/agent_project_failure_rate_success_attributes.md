## Pattern

Nearly 4 in 5 enterprises running AI agent pilots never reach production. The 12% that succeed share exactly four attributes:
1. **Pre-deployment infrastructure investment** made before the pilot
2. **Governance documentation** completed before pilots begin
3. **Baseline metrics** captured before deployment
4. **Dedicated business owner** with post-deployment accountability

## Evidence

Cross-referenced across Multimodal.dev, Axis Intelligence, Onereach.ai — multiple independent sources converge on the same 88% figure and the same 4 attributes.

For those who succeed:
- 171% average ROI (192% in the US)
- 540% median ROI in high-performance cohorts
- 47% productivity improvement across knowledge work
- Gartner: 40% of enterprise apps will include task-specific agents by end of 2026 (up from <5% in 2025)

## Decision Rule

The value proposition for Styfinity's AI OS should be explicitly: "we are the infrastructure and governance layer that puts clients into the 12%." Reframes the pitch — clients are not buying automation, they are buying access to the success cohort.

Internally: the 4 attributes must all be present before any workflow agent is deployed, or the rollout joins the 88%.

## Boundaries

- The 88% figure likely captures overly ambitious multi-system deployments
- Well-scoped single-workflow automations with clear reversibility have a much higher success rate
- The 4 attributes scale with project complexity
