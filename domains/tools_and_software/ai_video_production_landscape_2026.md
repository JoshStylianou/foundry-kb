# AI Video Production Landscape (April 2026)

**Confidence:** medium-high
**Source:** Primary web research across 25+ sources, April 2026
**Domain:** tools_and_software

## Pattern

AI video production tools in April 2026 are mature enough for faceless/explainer content but NOT for professional B2B talking-head content that builds authority with C-suite audiences. The realistic approach is hybrid: human records core content, AI handles production (editing, B-roll, captions, shorts extraction, SEO, thumbnails).

## Key Facts

- **HeyGen Avatar IV** is best-in-class for AI avatars ($29-99/mo) but attentive viewers can still detect synthetic quality
- **ElevenLabs** is best-in-class for voice cloning ($22/mo) — passes casual listening tests but real-world cost is ~2.8x advertised due to regeneration overhead
- **Descript** is the editing hub ($24-35/mo) — text-based editing, filler removal, eye contact fix, voice cloning built in
- **Opus Clip** is the repurposing standard ($15-29/mo) — auto-extracts shorts with virality scoring
- **PlayHT was acquired by Meta** (July 2025) and shut down Dec 2025 — no longer an option
- **YouTube cracked down hard** on AI content in Jan 2026 — thousands of channels suspended for "AI slop"
- **YouTube policy**: AI-assisted = fine, AI-replaced = must disclose, mass-produced AI = penalized
- **Recommended stack cost**: ~$155-205/mo for hybrid production (Descript + Opus Clip + TubeBuddy + vidIQ + Canva + Veo 3 + Claude API)
- **Open-source YouTube automation** (MoneyPrinter 50K stars, ShortGPT 7.2K) is faceless/shorts only — not suitable for B2B authority content
- **n8n workflow templates** exist for end-to-end pipelines (HeyGen + GPT-4 + YouTube upload) and are the most practical orchestration layer
