## Core Principle

Despite the hype around llms.txt as an emerging standard, AI crawlers overwhelmingly discover content through standard web pages and site structure, not /llms.txt files.

## Evidence

OtterlyAI study: only 0.1% of AI bot visits actually request the /llms.txt file.

## When to Apply

- Low priority to implement llms.txt for client sites
- Don't waste billable hours on it when structured data and standard crawlability deliver measurably higher AI citation rates
- If a client asks about llms.txt, the honest answer is: the data doesn't support prioritizing it

## Boundaries

- The standard is still evolving — adoption could increase if major AI providers begin requesting it
- Zero cost to generate one (Styfinity's /geo-llmstxt skill can do it), so it's not harmful — just not worth positioning as a value-add service
