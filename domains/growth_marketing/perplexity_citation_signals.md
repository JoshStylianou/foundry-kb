## Core Principle

Perplexity has distinct citation signals from ChatGPT (which relies on Bing rankings). Structured data and author verification are directly measurable levers.

## Evidence (April 2026)

- Publisher citation weight for structured data markup increased ~23%
- Verified expert authors get +15% citation preference
- Deep Research now uses Opus 4.6 (improved research quality)
- Memory engine: 95% recall (up from 77%), half as many false memories
- Model Council runs GPT-5.4, Opus 4.6, Gemini 3.1 Pro in parallel

## When to Apply

- Author bios with verifiable credentials + schema markup = measurable citation advantage on Perplexity specifically
- GEO strategy should differentiate between ChatGPT optimization (Bing-driven) and Perplexity optimization (structured data + author authority driven)
- For Styfinity clients: ensure author pages have full Person schema with knowsAbout and sameAs

## Boundaries

- Perplexity's market share is smaller than ChatGPT's — weight optimization effort accordingly
- Citation weight percentages are approximate, derived from changelog language rather than controlled study
