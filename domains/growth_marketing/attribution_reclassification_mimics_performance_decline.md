## Pattern

When an ad platform narrows its attribution definitions, reported conversion metrics drop without any change in actual ad performance or billing. The risk for agencies and performance marketers: clients see the drop first and conclude performance declined. The counter-pattern is proactive reclassification communication — explain the measurement change before clients see the numbers, rebaseline benchmarks, and add the new metric categories to dashboards before the next reporting cycle.

This is not a one-time event. Every major platform periodically redefines what counts as a conversion, click, or view. The pattern recurs: platform announces quietly, metrics shift in dashboards, clients panic, agencies scramble to explain retroactively. Proactive communication converts a trust crisis into a demonstration of expertise.

## Evidence

- **Meta March 3, 2026 attribution overhaul:**
  - Click-through attribution now counts link clicks only (likes, saves, shares, comments removed from click-through, moved to new "engage-through" 1-day window)
  - Engaged-view video threshold changed: 10 seconds to 5 seconds
  - Non-link conversions at days 2-7 disappear entirely from all reporting categories — this is true signal loss, not reclassification
  - Reported click counts expected to drop ~30-40% vs. prior baseline
  - Billing unchanged — advertisers pay the same, reports show less
  - Northbeam and Triple Whale updated their attribution models in response
- **Source corroboration:** Meta for Business official announcement, ALM Corp blog analysis, Leaf Signal breakdown, Jon Loomer Digital coverage — four independent sources reaching the same conclusion

## Application Trigger

Use this when any major ad platform updates its attribution methodology and you manage client accounts:
- Meta, Google, TikTok, or LinkedIn changes what counts as a click, view, or conversion
- A third-party measurement platform (GA4, Northbeam, Triple Whale) updates its attribution model
- Any reporting system redefines metrics that appear in client-facing dashboards

Also applies outside advertising — any context where a measurement system redefines what it measures: analytics platforms, financial reporting standards, performance benchmarking tools. The principle is the same: explain the ruler changed before people see the numbers.

## Boundaries

- Some reclassified conversions (Meta's non-link conversions at days 2-7) vanish with no re-categorization anywhere — this is genuine signal loss, not just reclassification. Proactive communication must distinguish between "we can still see this in a different column" and "this data no longer exists"
- Proactive communication only works if you know about the change before the client sees the data — requires monitoring platform announcements actively
- Over-communicating measurement changes can itself erode confidence ("why does everything keep changing?") — frame as industry evolution, not platform instability
- Does not apply when the metric drop reflects actual performance decline — misattributing real problems to measurement changes is worse than not explaining at all
