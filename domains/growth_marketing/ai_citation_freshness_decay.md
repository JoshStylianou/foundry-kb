## Core Principle

AI citation decay is real. Content freshness is a first-class ranking signal for AI search engines, with a much shorter half-life than traditional SEO.

## Evidence

- 50% of content cited in AI search is less than 13 weeks old
- Pages updated within 2 months get 28% more AI citations
- After 3 months, citation rates drop sharply

## When to Apply

- GEO content strategy requires a <90-day refresh cadence for any page targeting AI citation
- This must be baked into client content calendars as a standard deliverable — not "publish and forget" but "publish and refresh"
- Prioritize updating highest-value pages on a rolling 8-week cycle
- Service design: content refresh retainers are a natural recurring revenue stream for GEO engagements

## Boundaries

- Evergreen reference content (definitions, how-to guides) may retain citations longer if the underlying facts haven't changed
- Freshness signal likely varies by query type — breaking news queries weight recency more than educational queries
