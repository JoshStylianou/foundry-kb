## Core Principle

87% of ChatGPT search citations match Bing's top results. Brands that rank well on Google but not Bing are largely invisible in ChatGPT responses. Bing optimization is now mandatory for any GEO strategy.

## Evidence

Seer Interactive study (circulating April 2026): systematic comparison of ChatGPT web search citations against Bing SERP rankings. 87% correlation rate.

## When to Apply

- Every GEO engagement must include Bing optimization (Bing Webmaster Tools submission, Bing-specific indexing, Bing Places for local)
- Audit clients' Bing rankings alongside Google rankings
- Product data for e-commerce must be optimized for Bing to surface in ChatGPT shopping
- Bing Webmaster Tools is beta-testing an "AI Performance Report" tracking Copilot citation data — get on waitlist

## Boundaries

- Correlation study, not causal. ChatGPT may also use other signals not captured.
- Google-dominant markets may still weight Google optimization higher for overall traffic.
- Perplexity uses different citation signals (see perplexity_citation_signals entry).
