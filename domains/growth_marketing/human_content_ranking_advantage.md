## Core Principle

AI-generated content can reach Page 1 of Google but has a ceiling for position 1. Human-written content is 8x more likely to hold the top spot.

## Evidence

Semrush study (April 2026): systematic analysis of ranking pages by content origin. Human-written pages dominate position 1 at an 8:1 ratio. AI content appears in lower Page 1 positions.

## When to Apply

- Styfinity pitches: reinforces the value proposition of human-led content strategy augmented by AI, not replaced by it
- Content strategy: use AI for drafting/research/scale, but human expertise and editorial judgment for pages targeting position 1
- Good LinkedIn content data point for Josh's personal brand
- Client education: "AI content gets you on Page 1; human expertise gets you to #1"

## Boundaries

- Study methodology not fully public — "human-written" vs "AI-generated" classification criteria matter
- The gap may narrow as AI content quality improves
- Position 1 matters most for head terms; for long-tail, AI content in positions 3-7 may be perfectly adequate ROI
