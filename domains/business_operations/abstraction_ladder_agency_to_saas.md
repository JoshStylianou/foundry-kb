## Pattern

Each stage of business evolution involves going up one level of abstraction: custom work → templates/productized service → info products/community → SaaS. Each level multiplies leverage while reducing time dependency. The compounding effect is dramatic — same person, same domain knowledge, 10-20x revenue difference between stages.

The transition signal: when your audience shifts from "how do I use this tool?" to "how do I build what you built?" — you've crossed from practitioner to guide. That's when the next abstraction level becomes the higher-leverage play.

## Evidence

Saraev's documented revenue milestones:
- Custom agency (LeftClick): ~$25K/month
- Productized templates: $72K/month solo (30 min per project, $1K each — 11x labor productivity increase)
- Community (MMWM): $28/month launch → raised to $128/month, filled faster at higher price
- Combined: $100K/month profit (October 2024)
- Current (2026): ~$400K/month profit (~$4.8M annualized)

Productization framework: (1) Define deliverables as concrete outputs, (2) Map tasks backward from output, (3) Group by job role, (4) Calculate labor costs, (5) Sum to internal product cost, (6) Price using margin formula (cost / (1 - target margin)). Moved agency from 20% to 50% gross margins — 2.5x profitability.

## Apply When

- Planning business evolution for Styfinity, TNT Growth, or TraiderJ
- Recognizing the audience transition signal across any business
- Deciding when to invest in productization vs. keep customizing
- Pricing strategy for each stage

## Boundaries

- Need at least 5-10 similar client engagements before productization makes sense — premature productization locks you into the wrong template
- Each stage requires genuinely different skills (delivery → systems → content → software)
- Saraev's thesis: execution cost drops ~40x/year, commoditizing tactical automation knowledge within 24-36 months — the abstraction ladder accelerates as AI compresses lower levels
