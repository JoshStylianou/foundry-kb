## Positioning: Doctor, Not Pharmacist

**Pharmacist:** Client says "I need an AI chatbot" → you build a chatbot → you compete on price.

**Doctor:** Client says "I need an AI chatbot" → you diagnose the actual problem → you prescribe the right solution (which may not be a chatbot) → you compete on expertise.

The doctor position is defensible. The pharmacist position commoditises to zero.

## Pricing: Value-Based, Not Hourly

The build itself is commoditising — anyone with Claude Code can build automations. What isn't commoditising:

1. **Process decomposition** — understanding which processes to automate and in what order
2. **Edge case identification** — knowing where automations break before they break
3. **Ongoing optimisation** — tuning the system after deployment

**Pricing rule:** If a build saves the client $10K/month, charge $5K for it. The client gets 2x ROI from day one, and you capture value proportional to impact — not hours.

Never price by the hour. Hourly pricing punishes speed and rewards inefficiency.

## Career Path

```
Freelancer → Consultant → Trusted Partner
(builds what's asked) (diagnoses + builds) (embedded, strategic, recurring)
```

The goal is trusted partner: recurring revenue, strategic influence, and a position that can't be replaced by the next person who learns Claude Code.
