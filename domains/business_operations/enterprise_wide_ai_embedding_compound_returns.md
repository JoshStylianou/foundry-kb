## Core Principle

The performance variable separating organizations achieving meaningful AI returns from those running expensive pilots is breadth of integration across functions, not depth of any single deployment.

## Evidence

PwC 2026 Global CEO Survey (4,454 CEOs, 95 countries):
- Only 12% of CEOs have achieved both cost reduction AND revenue growth from AI
- This 12% is 2–3x more likely to have embedded AI extensively across products, demand generation, and strategic decision-making
- Companies applying AI to products and customer experiences achieved ~4 percentage points higher profit margins
- 56% of all CEOs have seen no significant financial benefit

## When to Apply

- Sequencing AI OS investments — data argues for wide shallow deployment before narrow deep specialization
- Evaluating whether a client's AI spend is structured for compound returns or isolated wins
- Pitching AI OS as cross-functional infrastructure, not point solutions

## Boundaries

Very early-stage implementations where establishing one reliable, trusted agent workflow is the correct first move. Companies without data infrastructure to enable broad embedding can't skip that foundation step.
