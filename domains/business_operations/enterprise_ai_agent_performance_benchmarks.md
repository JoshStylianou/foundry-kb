## Core Principle

First publicly available ROI benchmarks from a scaled, funded enterprise AI agent deployment provide concrete numbers for business cases.

## Evidence

Wonderful (enterprise AI agent startup, $150M Series B at $2B valuation, March 12, 2026):
- 60% reduction in handling times
- 80%+ containment rates (interactions handled without human escalation)
- Production multi-lingual deployments across telco, finance, healthcare, manufacturing
- 30+ markets
- Verified by Insight Partners, Index, IVP, and Bessemer at investment

## When to Apply

- Benchmarking client expectations for enterprise AI agent ROI
- Building business cases for agency AI OS implementations
- Validating whether a deployment is performing above or below market baseline

## Boundaries

Benchmark reflects customer service / operational use cases. Creative, strategic, and judgment-intensive tasks will have materially different performance profiles.
