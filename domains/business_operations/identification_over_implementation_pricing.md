## Pattern

In automation services, the highest-paid skill is identifying which automations are worth building — not building them. Build speed is table stakes (and collapsing further as AI accelerates implementation). The $5K-$50K engagements are won by people who walk into a business and immediately scope the right automation, not by people who can execute faster.

This means: lead with discovery/audit, not build proposals. Price the identification, not the implementation hours.

## Evidence

- Nate Herk: "$5K-$50K engagements" won by identification skill, not build speed
- AI-assisted build times collapsing (hours to minutes with MCP integrations) makes implementation a commodity
- The zero-code positioning of Herk's entire 10hr course confirms: the valuable skill is directing, not coding

## Apply When

- Pricing Styfinity automation consulting engagements
- Structuring client discovery — lead with audit, not proposal
- Hiring: look for business-process identification skills, not tool expertise
- Positioning against competitors who price on build hours

## Boundaries

- Only applies to B2B services where the client doesn't know what to build
- Consumer products are priced on output, not problem identification quality
- Still need implementation capability to deliver — just don't price it as the premium
