## Core Principle

When enterprises deploy AI faster than they can govern, the governance layer becomes a mandatory purchase — not a best-practice recommendation. The governance gap is not a blocker to AI OS adoption — it is the primary driver of governance-layer purchasing.

## Evidence

- 67% of business and IT decision-makers have felt pressured to approve AI deployments despite security concerns
- 1-in-7 described those concerns as "extreme" but overridden
- Only 38% of organizations have comprehensive AI policies
- AI Model Risk Management market growing $1.16B YoY in 2026 ($7.17B → $8.33B)

## When to Apply

Positioning any AI OS product — lead with governance and trust architecture, not capability. Identifying which enterprise clients need governance tooling bundled into an AI OS sale.

## Boundaries

Governance-led positioning fails for SMB/startup buyers who haven't yet experienced compliance pressure.
